About Industrial Apps

Built for people who work with technology.

Industrial Apps is an independent software portfolio focused on practical digital tools for the electrical and industrial fields.

The goal is to develop focused Android applications that help users calculate, understand equipment, learn technical concepts and approach troubleshooting in a structured way.
