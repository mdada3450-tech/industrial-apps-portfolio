Information may be processed by Google and its service providers as necessary to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, maintain security and comply with legal obligations. The developer does not sell users' personal information.

Third-party services apply their own security and retention practices to information they process. The developer does not specify or control Google's retention periods. You should consult Google's privacy documentation for details.
