## Motor Calc 3P — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 16 November 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Motor Calc 3P (the “App”).

### 1. Information entered in the App

The App uses values entered by the user to perform motor-related calculations and display results. Users should not enter sensitive personal information into calculation fields. The developer does not request account passwords or payment-card information to use the calculation features.

### 2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where enabled, ad personalisation. Depending on the device, settings, region and applicable consent requirements, this information may include:

- Advertising identifiers or other device identifiers;
- IP address and general device or network information;
- App interactions and information about ad views or clicks;
- Diagnostics and information used to detect invalid activity and measure advertising performance.

Google's processing is governed by Google's own terms and privacy documentation:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/)

Available advertising controls vary by device and region.

### 3. Analytics and other third-party services

Google AdMob is the advertising service identified for the App. The developer has not yet completed a technical audit of the current release's full software development kit (SDK) list. Any additional analytics, crash-reporting or diagnostic services found in that audit must be identified here, together with the information they process.

### 4. Sharing of information

Information may be processed by Google and its service providers as necessary to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, maintain security and comply with legal obligations. The developer does not sell users' personal information.

### 5. Data security and retention

Third-party services apply their own security and retention practices to information they process. The developer does not determine Google's retention periods. Please consult Google's privacy documentation for further details.

### 6. Children's privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Users should review the privacy and parental-control options provided by third-party services used by the App.

### 7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, software dependencies or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the web page linked above, with its effective date updated accordingly.

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Motor Calc 3P, contact the developer through the [ElectroTech Studio portfolio website](https://mdada3450-tech.github.io/industrial-apps-portfolio/) or the developer contact details shown on the App's distribution page.
